Review Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who can leave a review

Only people who have bought from edenrelics.co.uk. A few days after your order is sent, we email you a link for that order; you can also review it from your account if you're signed in. Each order can be reviewed once, and reviews left this way are marked "Verified purchase".

What we publish

Every genuine review, good or bad. We don't hold back or pick out reviews, and we don't edit what you write. Reviews go live as soon as they're posted. If a review contains a link, an email address or a phone number, it waits for a quick check first so we can keep spam and personal information off the site.

We may reply publicly to a review. We never offer discounts, gifts or anything else in return for reviews.

Fake reviews aren't allowed

Reviews must be your own honest experience of your order. We don't write, buy or commission reviews, and we don't let anyone else do so for us.

When a review is removed

We only hide a review if it:

  • is abusive or offensive
  • contains personal information, such as someone's address or phone number
  • is spam or advertising
  • isn't about the purchase

We keep a record of why any review was hidden. Being negative is never a reason to remove one.

Reviews from our other shops

Some reviews on our reviews page were left by customers who bought from us on other selling sites. They're shown separately under "Reviews from our other shops", in the customer's own words.

Questions

If you think a review breaks these rules, or you'd like your own review changed or removed, please contact us.
