Capture a label photo

Uploads the photo to the evidence archive and records it. Requires the tool’s image storage (R2) to be configured — otherwise the upload will fail.

Photos are checked against the capture standard before they are stored. Labels need at least 1200px on the long edge — fill the frame rather than cropping afterwards, because these images have to stay readable long after the listing has gone.

@if (needsZipOriginality(capType)) { }
Upload from the camera roll (back catalogue)

For garments that have already been and gone. These are stored as historical rather than held to the capture standard — an old, slightly blurry photo of a label is worth far more than no record at all, and it can never be retaken.

Each photo’s own date is read from it where present, so the archive knows roughly when that garment passed through the shop rather than only when you uploaded it. Historical photos are kept separate from properly-shot ones and never count towards meeting the capture standard.
